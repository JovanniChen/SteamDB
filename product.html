开发者的话：

为何要采用抢先体验这种模式？

“We believe the best way to build a deep, engaging survival crafting experience is to involve players as early as possible. Early Access allows us to gather valuable feedback on balancing, progression, onboarding, multiplayer, and long-term systems while continuing to expand the game together with the community.”

这款游戏的抢先体验状态大约持续多久？

“While it is difficult to provide an exact timeline, we currently anticipate that Romestead will remain in Early Access for approximately one to two years.

This timeframe allows us to continue expanding the game with new content and features while maintaining a sustainable pace of development and acting on community feedback.”

计划中的完整版本和抢先体验版本到底有多少不同？

“Our goal for the full version of Romestead is to deliver a more polished, expanded, and feature-rich experience compared to the Early Access launch.

Throughout Early Access, we plan to introduce additional biomes, bosses, progression tiers, points of interest, decorations, and expanded world content. We also plan to continue improving performance, balancing, overall polish, expanded accessibility, and full controller support.”

抢先体验版本的现状如何？

“We already feel the Early Access version of Romestead captures the fantasy of exploring, surviving, and building across the ruins of a fallen Roman world.

The Early Access version includes the core gameplay systems of Romestead, including exploration, combat, crafting, farming, town-building, progression across three distinct biomes, co-op multiplayer, and a procedurally generated world featuring handcrafted dungeons and points of interest.”

在抢先体验期间和结束之后，游戏价格会有所不同吗？

“Yes. We expect to gradually increase the price of Romestead as new content and features are added throughout Early Access and as we move closer to the full 1.0 release.”

在开发过程中，你们是如何计划让玩家社区参与进来的？

“Community involvement is an important part of our development process. We invite players to join our official Discord server to share feedback, report bugs, discuss balance, and suggest improvements directly with the team.

We will use community feedback to help prioritize improvements, quality-of-life features, balancing changes, and future content throughout Early Access.”

关于此游戏

一场末日灾难之后，罗马沦为废墟， 昔日市民竟重生为行尸走肉，在夜幕降临之后游走奔袭，稍有松懈就会沦为它们的口粮。快建造你的防御基地， 点燃火把，活下去！

重建文明 开疆拓土

物色好依山傍水的新定居地，建造铁匠铺、皮革坊、农场，并不断解锁更多设施！待这片居民吃饱喝足心情舒畅之后，你就可以安心开拓新的领地了

在这片罗马废墟，你得仰仗各行各业的匠人打造和升级你的小镇基地，所以不妨在小镇四周点缀些装饰物，让你的居民心情愉悦干活有劲。在不断的地图探索和研究后，你将解锁全新配方升级建筑和物流，摆脱人力劳作，实现生产线自动化拓荒新时代！

游戏特色

石头木材你可以捡起来,顶头上,放包里或者扔着玩，没什么做背包管理的必要！敌人贴脸了？扔他干嘛，愣着啊！

武器弹药准备充足了再出门，也别忘记带上美味的食物（红蘑菇）和其他消耗品获得增益。 等得到神的眷顾后，还可以升级各类技能以及解锁新天赋


罗马陨落之后，诸神便失去了绝大部分信徒，其神力也随之消弭。供奉祭祀神祇，让神明重返罗马荣光，注意每位神明所解锁的增益和技术都各有不同，你和好友的选择至关重要！

这片程序生成的世界广阔无垠，你要深入荒野采集资源，挖掘宝藏，探索生态迥异的各个区域，去解开坍塌帝国的终极秘密

召集你的小队即刻出发！ 解开地牢机关，搜刮战利品，找到深藏各地的强大boss，配合你和好友各自的独门绝技打败他们！
